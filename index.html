About us

We are Cat and Delight. Together, we're CatsDelight (no, Cat doesn't own Delight). What we share is the same instinct a cat has for a sunbeam or an open window: the urge to go find out what's over there. So we hike across Norway's mountains and forests chasing that very feeling, and we rarely photograph the view from the top. Somewhere on every trail, one of us gets sidetracked by a flower, an animal, a strange rock, or a bug doing something interesting. That's usually where the good photo turns up.

«Jeg fant jeg fant! Hva fant du nå da? Jeg fant noe kult å ta bilde av!»

I found it, I found it! What did you find? I found something cool to take a picture of!

Norwegian places. Norwegian hikes. And, when the trail is quiet enough, Norwegian ASMR.
